# Vannevar Bush & Memex

> **Category:** `person / historical concept`  
> **Milestone:** *As We May Think* (1945)

---

## 📋 Profile & Description
**Vannevar Bush** was an American engineer and science administrator who conceptualized the **Memex** (Memory Extender) in his seminal 1945 essay *As We May Think*.

---

## 🎯 Significance to the LLM Wiki Paradigm
- **Associative Trails:** Bush envisioned a personal desk/device where a user could store books, records, and communications, and link them with custom associative trails.
- **The Unsolved Problem:** Bush's Memex required intensive manual maintenance to create and sustain links. In the [[llm-wiki]] paradigm, the LLM solves the maintenance bottleneck that made personal associative trails burdensome for 80 years.
