# Persistent Compounding Knowledge

## 💡 Overview & Definition
**Persistent Compounding Knowledge** is a knowledge management property wherein every newly ingested document, reflection, or resolved query increases the contextual richness and connectivity of the entire knowledge base without requiring linear re-work.

---

## 🧩 Core Principles & Dynamics
1. **Compilation over Real-Time Derivation:** Raw information is extracted and integrated into structured concept and entity notes at ingest time rather than left unparsed in a database.
2. **Associative Trails:** Links between notes form a semantic web (inspired by [[vannevar-bush-memex]]), creating emergent insights when viewing Obsidian Graph View.
3. **Contradiction Resolution:** When a new source contradicts an existing claim, the system does not overwrite; it explicitly documents the tension, surfacing nuance over time.

---

## ⚖️ Trade-offs & Comparisons
- **Stateless Chat vs. Compounding Wiki:**
  - *Stateless Chat:* High friction, forgotten context, ephemeral insights lost in chat history.
  - *Compounding Wiki:* Insights filed into [[llm-wiki]], searchable, auditable, and compounding indefinitely.
