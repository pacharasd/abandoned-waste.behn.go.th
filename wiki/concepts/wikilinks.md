# WikiLinks

## 💡 Overview & Definition
**WikiLinks** (represented with double brackets such as `[[Target Page]]` or `[[Target Page|Custom Label]]`) are the foundational syntax used in personal knowledge bases like [[obsidian]] to create associative backlinks between documents.

---

## 🧩 Core Dynamics in LLM Wiki
1. **Bidirectional Association:** Linking from Page A to `[[Page B]]` automatically creates an inbound backlink on Page B.
2. **Graph Visualization:** In Obsidian, every WikiLink renders as a physical edge between two nodes in the Graph View.
3. **Resilience over Deep Directory Paths:** Obsidian resolves target note names dynamically across subdirectories without breaking even if files move.
